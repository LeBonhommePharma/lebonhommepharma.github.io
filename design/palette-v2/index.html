// palette · current state

Final palette — seven key colors, crayon-sourced

Built on 5a, and now sourced from the macOS crayon box throughout: a firetruck red #F5232B for temperature, Aqua #00A2FF for the vibrational term, Strawberry #FF2F92 for the receptor, and Magnesium for the baseline. Splitting T from ΔS means the equation can now be read term by term in color: cherry for the temperature scalar, violet for the entropy it multiplies. Contrast is measured against the ink so you can see which hues carry 10px mono labels and which are display-only. No yellow, no cyan, no salmon.

This is the ramp crystallographers already read — PyMOL colors the crystallographic temperature factor blue through white to red, so cool-to-hot needs no legend. It also keeps the equation's hues out of it: violet, mint and tangerine mean ΔS, ΔH and ΔG, and can't double as temperature stops without muddying both. Only the two anchors are key colors — aqua at the cold end, firetruck at the hot end, magnesium as the ambient reference — and firetruck stays the color of the T symbol itself.

The order is thermodynamic, not chromatic: the ramp walks the binding coordinate — apo baseline, unbound ligand where ΔS dominates, first pocket contact, vibrational rigidification, contacts formed where ΔH dominates, converged ΔG. Position carries energy, so a series legend reads as a reaction path. Firetruck stays out of it on purpose — it is a scalar and a failure signal, never a data class. Magnesium #DCDCE4 sits just under body text in brightness, so a reference line reads as neutral data without out-shouting the terms it is compared against.

// exploration 04 — extending the palette

Six key colors, each carrying a quantity

Not fixed — the palette goes from three to six, and each addition carries a real quantity rather than a mood. ΔH is mint, TΔS violet, ΔG tangerine; the three new ones are ΔS_vib (the tENCoM vibrational term, until now uncolored), the receptor and its pocket, and the baseline or apo reference. Yellow is gone from every layer, and the series ramp is ordered by energy rather than by hue — it walks the binding coordinate from apo baseline to converged ΔG.
