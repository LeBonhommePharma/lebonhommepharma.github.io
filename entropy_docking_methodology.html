Part I: Theoretical Foundation

1.1 The Central Thesis

Shannon entropy is scale-invariant. A bit is a bit whether measured from molecular configurational states, audio spectral distributions, cardiac R-R intervals, or vascular compliance waveforms. The rate of entropy change dI/dT (Shannon Energy Collapse rate) is therefore a universal observable that can be measured simultaneously at multiple biological scales using consumer hardware. When these rates correlate across scales, the system exhibits multi-scale thermodynamic closure — a state where information is conserved through a complete forward/reverse Crooks cycle.

ENTROPY DOCKING is the embodied proof-of-concept: a game/instrument that couples molecular docking thermodynamics (FlexAID∆S) to physiological biofeedback (NATURaL) through music (Apple Music) and spatial audio (AirPods), measured and controlled across the Apple device ecosystem.

1.3 The Crooks Round-Trip Game Mechanic

Crooks fluctuation theorem (Crooks 1999):

P(W_fwd) / P(−W_rev) = exp[β(W − ΔG)]

The forward and reverse work distributions cross at exactly W = ΔG. Bennett's acceptance ratio (BAR) exploits this for minimum-variance ΔG estimation.

In the game, a "cycle" consists of:

Forward (heating): Music tempo increases → BPM rises → T rises → ligand explores binding pocket → entropy increases → ANC fades toward Transparency → environmental noise enters → cardiac HRV increases → spatial audio field becomes disordered.

Reverse (cooling): Music tempo decreases → BPM drops → T drops → ligand converges on binding pose → entropy decreases → ANC engages → noise filtered → HRV decreases → spatial audio harmonizes into resonant chord.

The irreversible entropy production per cycle:

σ_irr = ⟨W_fwd⟩ + ⟨W_rev⟩ − 2ΔG ≥ 0

The game objective: minimize σ_irr. Perfect play achieves σ_irr → 0, meaning the cycle is thermodynamically reversible — zero information lost. The Landauer cost of each cycle is I_lost = σ_irr / (k_B ln 2) bits.

Why this is falsifiable: If the multi-scale entropy rates dI/dT do NOT correlate across scales during optimal play (σ_irr minimal), the framework is wrong. If they DO correlate with r > 0.5, the central thesis — that Shannon entropy is the universal bridge — is empirically supported by consumer hardware measurement.

The 100Hz IMU refresh rate for head tracking is identical between H1 and H2. Head tracking accuracy is equivalent. The Crown's haptic feedback resolution is the same.

2.3 Apple Watch Series 11: The Blood Pressure Channel

The Series 11 introduces oscillometric blood pressure measurement — cuff-validated, FDA-cleared for trend monitoring. This gives us a new thermodynamic observable that no previous NATURaL implementation had access to:

Blood pressure waveform P(t) = P_systolic × f(t) + P_diastolic

Vascular compliance C = dV/dP ∝ arterial elasticity

Vascular entropy: S_vasc = −Σ p(P_i) log₂ p(P_i)
  where P_i are binned pressure values over a cardiac cycle

Vascular temperature: T_vasc = MAP / k_vasc
  where MAP = (SBP + 2×DBP) / 3 = mean arterial pressure

This is the missing link between cardiac entropy (R-R intervals) and molecular entropy (configurational states). Vascular compliance is a MECHANICAL entropy — the artery wall's stiffness determines how many microstates the pressure wave can access. Young, compliant arteries = high S_vasc (many accessible pressure states). Stiff arteries = low S_vasc (constrained). This directly parallels receptor flexibility in FlexAID∆S: flexible receptor = high S_config, rigid receptor = low S_config.

The analogy that makes this work: Arterial compliance is to blood pressure what receptor flexibility is to binding free energy. Both are entropy-mediated: more accessible states = more entropy = weaker confinement (lower ΔG in binding, lower MAP in vasculature). The Watch S11 literally measures the physiological analogue of receptor plasticity.

Part VII: Why This Works (The Deepest Layer)

Every thermodynamic system has a Hamiltonian. Every Hamiltonian has a partition function. Every partition function has an entropy. The partition function doesn't care what the microstates are — molecular rotamers, audio frequency bins, R-R intervals, blood pressure values. It counts accessible states and computes −k_B Σ p ln p.

Temperature is the conjugate variable to entropy. When you change temperature, you change the entropy. The rate of that change — dS/dT — is the heat capacity at the molecular scale. We call it dI/dT at the information scale. Same math. Same units (bits/K when properly normalized). Same physics.

The AirPods Max Digital Crown rotating is, at the most fundamental level, the same operation as RE-DOCK sweeping temperature through the Van't Hoff decomposition. One moves a physical dial. The other moves a simulation parameter. Both change the number of accessible states in a system. Both produce a measurable entropy change. Both satisfy the second law.

The game asks: when a human couples to a molecular system through music and biofeedback, do the entropy dynamics at all scales obey the same Crooks theorem? If yes, information is truly scale-invariant and thermodynamics is information geometry. If no, the scales are decoupled and entropy is merely a convenient analogy, not a physical bridge.

Either answer is publishable. But one of them changes everything.
