Manifesto

The psychiatric system isn't broken — it was built for a different century. We're building the tools for this one.

Open-source. Thermodynamically rigorous. Unapologetically pro-drug in the clinical sense that matters: every molecule is a hypothesis about biology, and the stigma attached to psychoactives has killed more people than the molecules themselves.

From the statistical mechanics of ligand binding to the social mechanics of harm reduction, the physics is the same: systems seek lower free energy. Our job is to lower the barrier — algorithmically, pharmacologically, politically.

"Drug is ALWAYS an answer — sometimes the only one available."

— @BonhommePharma
