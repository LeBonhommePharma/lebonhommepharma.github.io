// profile

Computational pharmacologist working at the intersection of genetic algorithms and statistical mechanics. Main author of FlexAIDΔS, a genetic-algorithm docking engine that computes the full ΔG = ΔH − TΔS — the conformational entropy term included, recovering the correct binding mode where enthalpy-only scoring fails. Every result is reproducible by construction and shipped under Apache 2.0. Anti-prohibitionist; harm reduction as applied thermodynamics. Montréal.
