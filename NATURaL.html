// what the hell is this

Pharmacology as gameplay. Yoga as medicine. Science as punk.

NATURaL is an iOS/macOS Swift app that refuses to pick a lane. It's a harm-reduction platform disguised as an RPG dressed up as a wellness app — and it's entirely open source.

The PokéDrug framework treats psychoactive compounds as collectible entities with real pharmacological stats: receptor affinity, bioavailability, half-life, therapeutic index. You don't "just say no." You understand the mechanism, make informed choices, and track your relationship with substances the same way you'd track a Pokédex entry.

Stack that with guided chair yoga sessions — breath-cued, accessible, designed for bodies that have been through some shit — and a Shannon entropy collapse engine that continuously optimizes session sequencing. The result is something that actually meets you where you are.

Built by LP (@BonhommePharma), PhD candidate, founder of Le Bonhomme Pharma, and firm believer that drug is always an answer — sometimes the only one available.

NATURaL.swift — PokéDrug branch
// Catch your first compound
let psilocybin = PokéDrug(
name: "Psilocybin",
type: .serotonergic,
ki_5HT2A: 6.nM,
halfLife: 2.5.hours,
shannonCollapse: .enabled
)
// Start your session
await NATURaL .session(drug: psilocybin,
yoga: .chairFlow,
breathCue: .coherent4_6)
▶ Session optimal. Entropy: 0.47 bits

PokéDrug Framework

Every psychoactive compound in the database is a collectible entity with real pharmacological stats. Receptor binding profiles (Ki values), pharmacokinetics, therapeutic index, harm-reduction notes — all surfaced as an RPG stat card.

No moralizing. No "this drug is bad." Just mechanistic pharmacology presented with the same care you'd give a Charizard holographic.

Chair Yoga + Breath Cuing

Guided sessions built around chair-based movement — accessible to bodies managing pain, disability, or altered states. Every posture is paired with precise breath cues: inhale counts, exhale ratios, retention holds.

Coherent breathing (4-6 pattern), box breath, extended exhale — nervous system regulation baked into every flow. The app adapts session intensity to your current entropy state.
