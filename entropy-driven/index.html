THE FIRST-PRINCIPLES CONNECTION

A search algorithm loses uncertainty as it finds a basin. A molecular ensemble loses accessible microstates as binding constrains motion. Both can be written with Shannon’s functional; they are analogous because both describe probability—not because they are the same physical quantity.

05 / EVIDENCE

SAME FUNCTIONAL.
DIFFERENT PHYSICS.

Shannon’s functional is the mathematical bridge, not a licence to merge unlike quantities. Every layer below states what is computed, what the result can mean, and where the implementation lives.

SEARCH H

Optimizer diagnostic

A histogram of population CF scores, reported in nats. Collapse means the search has concentrated its score distribution; it is not a molecular entropy or a physical ∆S.

Model estimator

Boltzmann-like reweighting uses CF and an effective T*. Its F* is an internally defined free-energy analogue in proxy units—not a calibrated experimental binding free energy.

Relative unless calibrated
