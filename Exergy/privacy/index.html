Privacy policy

Effective 17 September 2026. Exergy 1.0, in preparation. Le Bonhomme Pharma, Montréal, Québec. Contact: lp@thebonhomme.com.

No Exergy account

We do not operate a login, analytics, or usage-ingestion server. App Store privacy: Data Not Collected.

Tokens

OAuth tokens and API keys stay in the Keychain on your devices. They are never written to CloudKit records. Optional iCloud Keychain sync of sign-ins is off by default and, when enabled, is Apple’s end-to-end Keychain.

iCloud meters

If you opt in, remaining-quota snapshots (provider, nickname, used percent, reset time, optional spend) sync through your private CloudKit database. Apple holds that database under your Apple ID. We cannot read it.

Providers you connect

GitHub, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, xAI, and OpenRouter process requests you authorize under their own policies. We never receive those tokens.

Website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. No analytics pixels. Email to lp@thebonhomme.com is processed by mail providers.
