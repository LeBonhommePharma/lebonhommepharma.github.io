Entropy Docking

Most docking engines chase enthalpy and call it a day. FlexAID∆S treats the collapse of Shannon entropy across the genetic-algorithm population — H(genes) → 0 — as the signal that says “this pose is real.”

Shannon entropy collapse

Each generation of the GA holds a population of candidate poses. We read the Shannon entropy of the gene distribution, H(genes) = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ. Early on it is near-maximal — the search is exploring everywhere. As selection pressure concentrates the population around the true binding mode, H collapses toward zero. That collapse — not a single lucky score — is the convergence criterion.

The binding free energy

The score FlexAID∆S minimizes is a full statistical-mechanics free energy — not a learned regression target:

∆Gbind = Hvct Nheavy − T∆Sshannon + T∆Svib
H_vct / N_heavy Enthalpic core. Voronoi contact-function energy, normalized per heavy atom so big greasy ligands don't win by size alone.
−T∆S_shannon Configurational entropy. The conformational freedom lost on binding, read directly from the collapse of H(genes). This is the term everyone else drops.
+T∆S_vib Vibrational entropy. Soft-mode (tENCoM) contributions — the residual jiggle of the bound complex. Binding stiffens some modes and frees others; this term keeps the books honest.
Read it out loud

“Pack the pocket (enthalpy), pay the conformational rent (Shannon entropy), then refund whatever vibrational freedom the complex keeps.” Three terms, no black box.

Why entropy decides

In hydrophobic binding, the entropy term routinely dominates the enthalpy term. Squeeze ordered water out of a greasy pocket and the system gains entropy — a favorable −T∆S that can be larger than the entire enthalpic contribution. Score enthalpy alone and you rank the wrong pose, the wrong ligand, the wrong series.

The deep-learning blind spot

DL scorers learn a mapping from a single static complex to a number. Ensemble entropy is a property of the whole population of accessible states — it isn't in any one structure to learn from. A network can memorize that HSP90 binders score well; it cannot reason about the −T∆S that makes them bind. FlexAID∆S computes it from physics, every run.

Make Entropy Great Again

For thirty years, docking quietly deleted the hardest term in thermodynamics because it was inconvenient to compute.

Entropy got demoted to a fudge factor — a constant, a hand-wave, a penalty someone bolted on after the fact. Meanwhile every wet-lab ITC trace screamed that −T∆S was running the show. We think that's backwards. So we put entropy back where it belongs: at the center of the objective function, computed honestly from the genetic algorithm's own statistics.

No apologies, no asterisks. The same irreverence, the same rigor: a real free energy with a real entropy term, validated against 95.3% binding-mode recovery on Astex Diverse 85 (81/85, cross-docking, v79). Make entropy great again — and watch the wrong poses fall away.
