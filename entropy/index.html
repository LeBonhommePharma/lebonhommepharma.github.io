01 / THE COMMON LANGUAGE

Count the possibilities.
Keep their weights.

Entropy measures how probability is spread over a declared set of alternatives. A single dominant state has low entropy. Many equally weighted states have high entropy.

The same normalization and entropy calculation works for molecular states and token choices. The state space, the origin of the weights, and their physical meaning remain domain-specific.

First define the alternatives. Then define their measure. Only then interpret their entropy.

03 / TWO INSTANTIATIONS

The algebra travels.

MOLECULAR SEARCH

FlexAID∆S

From a collection of poses to a weighted ensemble.

aᵢ = −βEᵢ
β = 1 / (κT)

For individually represented, unit-multiplicity states with calibrated energies, Z sums their Boltzmann weights. The canonical free-energy identity balances the mean energy against entropy:

F = −κT ln Z = ⟨E⟩ − TS
S = κHnats

Use κ = kB for per-particle energies, or R for molar energies. A populated basin can matter even when it does not contain the single lowest-energy pose.

What has to be established

Calibrated energies, a defined state-space measure, and adequate sampling. A docking contact score passed through softmax is a score-space proxy. A binding ΔG additionally requires a matched association cycle; it is not the free energy of an arbitrary pose list.

TOKEN DISTRIBUTIONS

Shannon

From next-token alternatives to an uncertainty trace.

aᵢ = zᵢ / τ
pᵢ = softmax(z / τ)ᵢ

At a fixed context, a model’s logits zᵢ define relative weights over token choices. Their entropy describes concentration in that predictive distribution.

Hbits = −∑ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ
Neff = 2Hbits

The effective state count is the number of equally likely alternatives with the same entropy. The analogy zᵢ = −Eᵢ is mathematical; a token logit does not acquire thermodynamic energy units.

What has to be established

The probability support, normalization, and treatment of omitted tokens. A top-k feed does not establish full-vocabulary entropy. A sharp prediction can still be confidently wrong.

Grouped microstates: why multiplicity matters +

If record i represents nᵢ microstates of equal energy, use aᵢ = ln nᵢ − βEᵢ. Entropy across records and entropy across microstates are then different:

S / κ = Hrecords,nats + ⟨ln n⟩

The interactive example sets every nᵢ = 1. Grouping states without the within-group contribution changes the entropy being reported.

SHANNON / TOKEN STREAM

A drop against a rolling mean

The current C++ default compares Ht against an eight-sample rolling mean that includes the current sample. A full window and δt < −3.2 bits trigger the detector.

δt = Ht − H̄window

The threshold is in bits. A z-score is computed separately. This is a change detector, not the same test as the docking histogram thresholds.

Relabel the states and entropy stays the same—even if the dominant answer becomes wrong. Low entropy alone cannot establish a correct docking pose, truthful reasoning, deception, or evaluation awareness. Those claims need independent ground truth.
