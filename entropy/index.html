Two implementations. Two different tests.+

FlexAID∆S watches occupancy across energy-score bins in a search population. Its 2-bit and 1-bit thresholds depend on the histogram support; the source records historical 256-bin versus current 20-bin use.

Shannon compares the current entropy with an eight-sample rolling mean, including the current sample. Its default drop threshold is −3.2 bits. The z-score is a separate quantity.

This eight-state experiment has at most 3 bits of entropy. It does not trigger or reproduce either repository’s detector.
