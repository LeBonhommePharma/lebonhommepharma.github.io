About Le Bonhomme Pharma

A Montréal-based open-science lab run by Louis-Philippe Morency (PhD candidate, Université de Montréal, NRGlab). We build tools at the intersection of thermodynamics, computation, and biology — where entropy is the signal, not the noise.

All software is Apache 2.0 — free as in freedom, free as in beer. No GPL dependencies. The shared mathematical identity across our tools: ΔG = ΔH − TΔS.

Affiliated with NRGlab at the Université de Montréal. Collaborating with Rafael J. Najmanovich.
