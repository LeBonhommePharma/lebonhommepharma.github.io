Iargument

Dropping ΔS is not a simplification. It is a wrong answer.

Docking software has spent thirty years pretending that binding is an enthalpy problem. It is not. A ligand entering a pocket loses translational freedom, loses rotational freedom, freezes torsions, and evicts an ordered shell of water that was perfectly comfortable where it was. All of that is entropy, all of it is paid for at body temperature, and none of it appears if your scoring function is a sum of contact terms with a regression coefficient stapled on the end.

The field drops the term because it is expensive, then calls the omission a modelling choice. It is not a modelling choice. It is a number you did not calculate, reported as though you had.

FlexAIDΔS computes it: Shannon entropy over the search population for the configurational part, torsional ENCoM normal modes for ΔSvib, Voronoi contact complementarity for ΔH, resolved at 310.15 K into a ΔG you can argue with. That is the run printing itself out above. The five sections that follow are the five remaining stages of the coordinate it walks. It is slower than skipping it. It is also the equation.

Why Shannon, and what it costs

The same kernel — H = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ — reads pose-ensemble occupancy in a docking run, next-token entropy in an LLM agent, and band structure in heart-rate variability. One estimator, three state spaces. That is not a metaphor; it is the same integral with different records underneath it.

Honest accounting: the entropy path is not free, and a run that computes it will not win on wall-clock against a tool that skips it. The comparison worth making is against the right answer, not against the fast one. The kernel is walked through in full here →

02 unbound · ΔS violet · configurational entropy H = −Σ pᵢ log₂ pᵢ

Energy collapse is the metric, so here is the metric collapsing.

A search population starts spread across the score landscape and ends concentrated in one basin. The entropy of that occupancy is the thing FlexAIDΔS watches, and it is the same estimator Shannon points at a language model's next-token distribution. One kernel, two state spaces, thresholds stated rather than implied.

H is recomputed from the bars on every frame with the same kernel the engine uses — if the readout ever disagrees with the histogram, the histogram is right and the code is wrong. Note the ceiling: this is the entropy of the binned occupancy, so it tops out at ln 20 = 2.996 nats, not at the terminal's unbinned S₀ = ln 128 = 4.852. Grouping states without the within-group contribution changes the entropy, which is a real caveat and not a rounding one. The dashed magnesium outline is the apo-baseline distribution the run started from.

Entropy is not a penalty term bolted onto a score. It is a statement about how many arrangements the system still has available, and it is the only part of the free energy that knows the difference between a pocket that fits one pose and a pocket that tolerates twenty.

A population that collapses too fast has stopped searching; one that never collapses has not converged. The detector cares about the shape of that curve, not about its endpoint — which is why the panel above prints S₀ → S and not a rank.

What the two implementations actually test

FlexAIDΔS watches occupancy across energy-score bins in a search population; its thresholds depend on the histogram support, and the source records historical 256-bin versus current 20-bin use. Shannon compares the current entropy with an eight-sample rolling mean including the current sample, with a default drop threshold of −3.2 bits; the z-score is a separate quantity.

They are two different tests on two different state spaces, and the figure beside this paragraph reproduces neither detector — it has at most ln 20 nats of headroom and is a demonstration of the kernel, not of the threshold.

IIargument

The stigma has killed more people than the molecules.

This is not a slogan, it is an epidemiological claim, and it is testable. Prohibition does not remove a drug from a population. It removes the dose from the drug — it hands supply to people with no reason to be accurate, and accuracy is the only thing standing between a person and an overdose.

Fentanyl did not arrive because anybody wanted fentanyl. It arrived because potency-per-gram is the single variable a prohibited supply chain optimises. Then the same policy makes it dangerous to ask for help, dangerous to carry a test strip, dangerous to be seen. Every one of those is a mechanism. Every one has a body count attached.

So publish the pharmacology instead. Kᵢ values, half-lives, metabolite routes, interaction lethality. Adults can read a binding table — they have been reading them here for eighty-two entries.

IIIargument

Mechanism over moralising. No exceptions.

A drug is a ligand with a binding profile, a metabolic fate and an interaction set. Cocaine is a rigid tropane that locks DAT in the outward-open conformation. MDMA is the pharmacological inverse — a substrate that rides the transporter inward and runs it backwards. One blocks, one reverses. Knowing which is which is not trivia; it is the difference between a hazard you can manage and one you cannot see coming.

That is the entire editorial policy of Drug of the Day. Full binding tables. Real co-crystals, named by PDB code and loadable in the next section, and plainly labelled when no pocket structure exists rather than shown as a plausible substitute. Clinical tone on risk. Zero "just say no", because "just say no" has never once prevented an overdose and a half-life has.

Which compounds, and why the boring ones matter most

Eighty-two entries: stimulants, opioids, psychedelics, benzodiazepines, antipsychotics, SSRIs, and the unglamorous ones. The unglamorous ones matter most — GABAA positive allosteric modulators plus an opioid kill more reliably and more quietly than anything on the illegal list, and nobody writes a moral panic about a repeat prescription.

    04 rigidification · ΔSvib aqua · tENCoM normal modes ΔS = ΔS_conf + ΔS_vib

    The complex stiffens as the network tightens, and ΔG pays for it.

    The terminal re-rolls its thermodynamics on every pass, and its ΔH swings across ten kcal·mol⁻¹ while its ΔG barely moves. That is not a bug in the generator; it is the thing the generator was written to respect. This figure is that distribution.

    fig. 4 — enthalpy–entropy compensation tui.js · S_B0 14.832 · S_B1 1.8056 ● PLOTTING
    10.00ΔH span · kcal·mol⁻¹
    4.40ΔG span · kcal·mol⁻¹

    −6.45ΔS_vib at ΔH −16.0

    ΔH and ΔS are not independent in real binding data: a tighter enthalpic network is paid for in conformational freedom, so the two drift together and ΔG varies far less than ΔH does. Every point is a real draw from tui.js's own generator, replayed with its own seeded mulberry32 — this is the distribution the terminal is sampling. Ten kcal·mol⁻¹ of enthalpy buys four and a bit of free energy; the rest is handed straight back. The dashed aqua line is ΔSvib = 0.75 + 0.45·ΔH, the component of ΔS the complex pays for stiffening. The tangerine marker is this page's illustrative pair, which sits below the line — two figures from two places, stated rather than quietly reconciled.

    ΔSvib is not a third subtraction. It is a component of ΔS: the complex rigidifies on binding, so it is negative, and it deepens as the enthalpic network tightens. Whatever is left once it is taken out is the configurational remainder, which keeps ΔS = ΔSconf + ΔSvib exact and spends no extra roll on it.

    This is the one place two of the three concepts this page merges disagreed with each other, and the code settled it — the panel above prints the decomposition, so the page prints the decomposition.

    The compensation slope, and why a straight line would be a lie

    S_B1 is set so that T·dΔS/dΔH ≈ 0.56: a little over half of any enthalpic gain is handed straight back as entropy, which is where measured compensation slopes for drug-like binding sit. The ±1.6 cal·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹ scatter band is what keeps this a correlation instead of a rule. A perfectly straight line would be its own kind of lie, and rolling ΔH and ΔS independently would scatter ΔG across a range no calorimeter has ever reported.

    05 contacts formed · ΔH mint · Voronoi contact function Vcontacts · interface by atom-type pair

    Enthalpy is not a distance cutoff. It is an interface.

    Most scoring functions count pairs inside a radius and call it contact. FlexAIDΔS tessellates instead: every atom gets the region of space closer to it than to any other, two atoms are in contact exactly when their regions share a face, and the enthalpy is the shared area weighted by atom-type pair. No cutoff, no double counting, and a buried atom cannot be in contact with something it is screened from.

    fig. 5 — Voronoi contact function 22 centres · half-plane clipping ● TESSELLATING
    0interface · units of length
    0contact pairs
    0ligand cells · units²

    Drag the tightness and the tessellation is recomputed, not replayed: the cells are rebuilt by clipping the frame with one half-plane per neighbouring centre, and the numbers above are measured off the polygon edges you can see. This is a 2-D schematic of a 3-D quantity and says so — Vcontacts sums interface areas between Voronoi polyhedra, this sums edge lengths between polygons. The mechanism is the same and the dimensionality is not.

    The useful property is that the interface is a partition. Every bit of a ligand atom's surface belongs to exactly one neighbour, so the contributions sum to the whole and nothing is counted twice. Tighten the pocket and the mint edges thicken; loosen it and the ligand starts sharing faces with the solvent instead.

    That is also why ΔH here is mint and nothing else on this page is. One colour, one quantity — the contact-pair count beside it is strawberry because a pair is a receptor fact, and the ligand's own share of space is magnesium because a volume is a baseline measurement and not an energy.

    What the real implementation does that this schematic does not

    The engine works on a 3-D additively-weighted tessellation with atomic radii, over a 40 × 40 SYBYL atom-type matrix, batched across poses. The complementarity term is a sum over type pairs of the shared area times a matrix coefficient, which is where the soft in "soft complementarity" lives: a pair that should not be adjacent is penalised by area rather than rejected by distance.

    This figure has no radii, no atom types and one fewer dimension. It is here to make the word "Voronoi" mean something specific rather than to stand in for the calculation.

    06 converged · ΔG tangerine · free energy ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

    Enthalpy buys binding. Entropy takes its cut, and the cut scales with temperature.

    The end of the coordinate. Drive the temperature and watch the identity re-resolve: two constants go in — ΔH and ΔS, both illustrative, both stated — and every other number on these two instruments is computed from them, including the argument at the bottom.

    fig. 7 — ΔG(T), and the penalty it pays 200 → 350 K ● TRACKING

    The gap between the two lines is −TΔS, and the slope of ΔG is −ΔS. Nothing here is drawn from a design file: both lines are the same two constants the ledger uses, evaluated across the range. Warm the system and the penalty widens; that widening is the entire reason a 298 K bench number does not transfer to a 310 K body.

    See the arithmetic, and what it does not claim

    ΔH = −16.0 kcal·mol⁻¹  ·  ΔS = −22.0 cal·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹
    −TΔS = −T · ΔS / 1000 = +0.0220 · T
    ΔG = ΔH − TΔS = −16.0 + 0.0220 · T  →  −9.18 at 310 K
    ΔSvib = 0.75 + 0.45 · ΔH = −6.45 cal·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹
    ΔSconf = ΔS − ΔSvib = −15.55 cal·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹

    The unit trap, named so it cannot come back. ΔH and ΔG are kcal·mol⁻¹. ΔS is cal·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹ — the convention entropies are tabulated in — so T·ΔS lands in cal·mol⁻¹ and must be divided by 1000 before it can be subtracted from an enthalpy. Miss that and every ΔG is off by three orders of magnitude while still looking plausible.

    Where ΔSvib comes from, and where it does not. The coefficients 0.75 + 0.45·ΔH are tui.js's own V_B0 and V_B1, applied at this page's ΔH. They live on the terminal's compensation line, and this page's published pair (−16.0 / −22.0) does not sit on that line — the relation would put ΔS near −14.06 at ΔH = −16.0. Fig. 4 plots both, which is why the tangerine marker there is below the violet line rather than on it.

    What this is not. Two held constants and a temperature sweep. No pose was docked to produce them, no receptor was scored, and nothing here is a measurement. The identity is real; the numbers are an illustration of it, and the one percentage on this page is arithmetic on those two constants. No success rate, RMSD, rank or benchmark figure appears anywhere until the campaign finishes.

    IVargument

    Correctness requires an independent reference.

    There is a great deal of money in announcing that a neural network has solved binding affinity. It has not. It has learned the distribution of its training set, which in structural biology means it has learned which proteins crystallographers found tractable thirty years ago. Ask it about a pocket nobody has deposited and it answers with total confidence and no information. Correlation without mechanism is astrology with better marketing.

    The alternative is not a better model. It is a checkable one. Proprietary docking licences survive because their outputs are unfalsifiable by anyone outside the licence — a convenient property for a vendor and a fatal one for a field. So everything here ships open: engine, protocol, seeds, receipts. Every run writes an engine SHA and a settings block next to the poses, which is why every figure above names its generator rather than asserting itself.

    Take a target we have never touched. Run it. Diff your receipt against ours. If the numbers disagree, that is a finding and it belongs in the open — which is the only arrangement under which any of this is science rather than advertising.

    On benchmark figures, and why none appear on this page

    The Astex campaign is still running. A number quoted before it finishes is a claim nobody has measured, and a number that wobbles on reload is worse — live-looking telemetry makes a fabricated figure more convincing, not less. The protocol is published now so the run is reproducible before the claim is made rather than after it is challenged. The figures will appear here with their receipts.
