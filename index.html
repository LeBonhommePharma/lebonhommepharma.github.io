Free-energy ledger. With ΔH held at −16.0 kcal/mol and ΔS at −22.0 cal per mol per kelvin, the entropy penalty −TΔS grows from +4.4 to +7.7 kcal/mol as temperature rises from 200 K to 350 K, so ΔG moves from −11.6 to −8.3 kcal/mol. Enthalpy buys binding; entropy takes its cut, and the cut scales with temperature.

Operating principles

The Physics First

01 Thermodynamics

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

Every binding event is a thermodynamic equation. Ignoring the entropy term isn’t a shortcut — it’s a wrong answer. We compute the whole thing.

02 Open science

Apache 2.0, Full Stop

Proprietary docking tools gatekeep science behind paywalls. Drug discovery belongs to everyone. Our code ships under Apache 2.0, reproducible by construction.

03 Harm reduction

Education Over Prohibition

The war on drugs failed the pharmacology exam. Psychoactives are tools with mechanisms — understandable, predictable, teachable. We teach them.

04 Mechanistic rigor

No Black Boxes

ML correlation without mechanism is astrology with better marketing. We use neural networks as a last resort, not a first instinct. First principles, every time.

Manifesto

The psychiatric system isn’t broken — it was built for a different century. We’re building the tools for this one.

Open-source. Thermodynamically rigorous. Unapologetically pro-drug in the clinical sense that matters: every molecule is a hypothesis about biology, and the stigma attached to psychoactives has killed more people than the molecules themselves.

From the statistical mechanics of ligand binding to the social mechanics of harm reduction, the physics is the same: systems seek lower free energy. Our job is to lower the barrier — algorithmically, pharmacologically, politically.

“Drug is ALWAYS an answer — sometimes the only one available.”
